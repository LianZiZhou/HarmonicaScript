- DryWetMidi's Playback / IOutputDevice / Multimedia spine, and with it HighPrecisionTickGenerator, free seek/pause/Speed/NoteCallback, and the native blobs. Cut because it computes live output on a different code path from the exported macro — the exact divergence the layered architecture exists to prevent — and because recompiling the timeline for a seek or speed change takes single-digit milliseconds anyway.
- A hand-rolled SMF parser. Cut because ExcludeAssets="build" solves the native-blob problem in one attribute, trimming is off anyway, and DryWetMidi's tolerant ReadingSettings, Sanitize and multi-tempo TempoMap are thousands of lines of correctness on exactly the malformed community files this project targets.
- Seam alternation as a feature, and the milestone gate attached to it. Cut because it is refuted by the instrument's own algebra: within a fixed modifier state at most one key renders any offset, so every alternative spelling costs a modifier transition, and under the shipped default profile alternating (40 ms) is WORSE than retriggering (32 ms). The DP will find it in the rare case it helps; there is no claim and no acceptance test.
- SolverIterations = 2 and the P14→P19→P14 feedback loop. Cut because the pessimistic clamped availSil makes the DP's feasibility judgement a sound lower bound realisable by local truncation alone, so the plan is correct in one pass. A proof replaces a hoped-for empirical convergence result.
- The transposition candidate prefilter (top-24 by cheapScore). Cut because it saves nothing at these sizes, prunes on the flat axis while the spiky axis decides the answer, mispriced the default fold policy by up to 20x on precisely the near-optimal candidates, and introduced a 'best 25 with ties' determinism hole.
- The 300 ms maximum key-hold cap that re-presses held keys in the scheduler. Cut because it mutates the stream below the validator (so the injected events are not the events that were golden-filed and simulated), contradicts the plan's own 'an all-sharp piece is one press and one release' costing, protects the less dangerous surface if scoped to keys, and bounds nothing after a hard kill. Replaced by compile-time hold caps expressed IN the IR plus the release-all batch at session start.
- The 150 ms FoldRepeatedNotes pass. Cut because at 150 ms it flattens every repeated sixteenth at ≤100 BPM and every repeated eighth at ≤200 BPM — i.e. repeated-note melodies across the pop/vocal files that are the modal use case — and it is redundant with the articulation ladder's merge rung, which fires only when retrigger is genuinely infeasible.
- Clamp-to-edge as a default out-of-range policy, and the 'drop if it would be the third consecutive identical pitch' sub-rule. Cut because clamping breaks pitch class and produces unison plateaus that read as a stuck note, which is strictly worse than an octave displacement, and because permitting two consecutive clamped unisons is exactly the artifact the rule was written to prevent.
- The 'write a READER for every writer, round-trip equality substitutes for the vendor software' mandate. Cut because reader and writer share one mental model, so a Razer <State> polarity inversion, a delay-before-vs-after error or an unescaped brace round-trips perfectly and ships — raising confidence in an unverifiable surface for no evidential reason. Replaced by decode fixtures against the real vendor samples, targeted golden bytes, and executing the .ahk on Windows CI.
- The exporter template DSL and the drop-in %LOCALAPPDATA%\exporters plugin registry. Cut because it grants an exemption from all three validation techniques to precisely the exporters nobody has ever seen, invents a mini-language with no error-reporting design, adds a load-arbitrary-config-from-a-writable-directory vector to a tool already expecting AV heuristics, and solves the wrong bottleneck — new vendors are blocked on sample acquisition, not on authoring speed. The capabilities JSON and the typed Quirks (where the unverified inferences actually live) are kept.
- The full fixtures x exporters golden cross product (400+ files, tens of MB of generated XML). Cut because it is the suite someone blanket-accepts or deletes at month six, defeating its own purpose. Replaced by golden lowered-event-lists for every fixture plus exporter bytes for six chosen ones.
- `hsc selftest --find-floor` (binary-searching noteHoldMinMs against Notepad readback). Cut because Windows imposes no meaningful minimum hold for a down/up pair to produce a character, so it would bottom out near 1 ms and write measured:true into the very field whose honesty badge is the point.
- The calibration WIZARD that auto-plays probe notes in-game. Cut because it presupposes Branch A already works, which is circular. Replaced by docs/CALIBRATION.md's six scripted experiments (each a golden-filed timeline plus one yes/no question), a TimingProfile editor, and profile import with a validating field diff.
- Probing the game process's integrity level via OpenProcess. Cut because it is the one cross-process behaviour ACE advertises intercepting and the information gain does not justify it; our own elevation state plus the Notepad delivery test plus honest 'this rung is unresolved' messaging covers the case.
- The requireAdministrator manifest and any auto-elevation. Cut because standing elevation is both a detection heuristic and a PRC-law aggravating factor; elevation is offered only as a user-initiated recovery action from inside the wizard.
- Auto-update, Velopack, telemetry, crash reporting, any cloud, any account, any bundled or shared song library, any score-sharing feature. Cut permanently for posture (a firewall check should show zero traffic, and Policy.Tests enforces it), for copyright exposure, and because a community score repo is the single feature that creates legal risk AND the popularity that attracts a per-tool detection signature.
- SharpHook, and cross-platform global hotkeys with it. Cut because its bundled native libuiohook ships GPL-3.0/LGPL-3.0 and would attach relinking obligations to an MIT distribution — the one contamination trap in the whole dependency graph.
- Native AOT and trimming on shipped artefacts. Cut because NativeAOT cannot cross-compile macOS→Windows, which would hard-bind the release pipeline to hardware the developer does not have. AOT-clean discipline is kept because it is free and it makes a missing [JsonSerializable] fail loudly in a Mac test rather than silently on a user's box.
- Avalonia 12, Avalonia.Headless.XUnit and all automated UI tests, Avalonia.Controls.TreeDataGrid (paid), and Avalonia's paid Hot Reload. Nothing here needs a paid Avalonia component, and skipping Headless removes the xunit v2/v3 pin conflict entirely.
- All in-app live audio output — NAudio (no macOS backend), Silk.NET.OpenAL, SDL2, OpenTK. Replaced by offline WAV rendering plus the OS default player: one fewer dependency class, one fewer platform-specific failure mode, and a deterministic CI-runnable audition path. The WAV is never byte-compared; SimulatedNote[] is golden-filed instead.
- Unseeded humanisation jitter, and any framing of jitter as detection avoidance. Ships off, seeded when on, named musically. Cut in its unseeded form because it would destroy every golden file the test strategy rests on, and cut in its evasive framing because that is a false safety claim to users and evidence of intent.
- Joint DP over out-of-range fold alternatives. The (realisation x state) node set would be ~31 wide instead of 6 for ~25x the runtime, to optimise the fingering of notes that are already musically compromised. Available as an off-by-default JointDP option.
- Interactive piano-roll editing — note insertion, drag-to-move, multi-select, rhythm and tempo editing, quantise-selection. The score is monophonic, so it is a list; a DataGrid plus a read-only strip carries the information at a fraction of the cost. Per-note key/octave/accidental change, delete/mute, PromoteChordSibling and undo ARE in, because user decision #6 settles them.
- The .hsq jianpu text IMPORTER (export only in v1), MML import, MusicXML, ABC, Sky Studio and DoMiSo import. MusicXML's .NET support is a wasteland, the audience reads jianpu rather than ABC, and MIDI in plus MIDI out is the whole interoperability story. MML is a cheap v2 addition that unlocks the Mabinogi/FFXIV corpus.
- Corsair iCUE .cueprofile — deferred with a stated re-entry condition, because cereal's polymorphic_id/ptr_wrapper bookkeeping plus drifting cereal_class_version numbers means a wrong file can corrupt a user's entire profile list. It is the one exporter whose failure mode harms the user rather than merely failing.
- VIA / Vial / QMK / ZMK as SONG exporters (~30-50 notes fit the macro buffer; Vial silently truncates), SteelSeries Engine/GG (no macro import at all), Razer Synapse 4 as a separate target (its Makecode interpretation rests on one 2024 beta bug report), and every unverified vendor including the entire Chinese budget cluster — AULA / VGN / AJAZZ / Dareu / Rapoo / Attack Shark / KZZI / 机械师 / MIIIW / ThundeRobot / ASUS / MSI / Cooler Master / ROCCAT / Glorious / Wooting. Zero verifiable formats between them, and two circulating 'specs' traced to AI-generated SEO farms. No speculative writers, ever.
- Keyboard Maestro, xdotool and ydotool exporters, and Linux packaging beyond a tar.gz — no AppImage, no .deb, no Flatpak. The game is Windows-only, so a Linux user can only do offline conversion and export.
- Additional instrument profiles (Genshin lyre, FFXIV Bard, 燕云十六声) in v1. The schema is generic and loopback.text.v1 ships as a second profile, but authoring a real second instrument means a second untestable surface before the first is verified.
- The 'out of scale / notes not in the diatonic scale' metric that every lyre tool reports as its headline quality number. Structurally always zero on a fully chromatic instrument. Reporting it would be theatre; the honest dominant metrics are out-of-range, octave-fold, modifier churn and mechanical deficit.
- Popularity-gating any settled requirement. The editor, the journal, .hsproj and the rehearsal view are scheduled milestones, not thresholds waiting on five strangers to volunteer an opinion.
